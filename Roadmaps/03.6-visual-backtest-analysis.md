# Phase 3.6 — Visual Backtest Analysis Roadmap

## Objective

Turn a completed persisted Backtest run into an inspectable research view without changing strategy logic. The user must be able to see what the strategy actually did, when it was exposed or flat, how the underlying moved, and how the strategy compared with a constant buy-and-hold benchmark.

Phase 3.6 is visualization/analysis only. It does not change the Phase 3 static-grid rules, simulator fill rules, Paper runtime, or Live execution.

## Benchmark contract

- Every completed Backtest view includes a **Buy & Hold** benchmark of the same underlying asset.
- Benchmark period is exactly the run's effective active Backtest period.
- Benchmark starts with the same initial capital as the strategy.
- Benchmark buys the underlying at the first active replay candle open and then holds continuously to the end.
- Benchmark equity at each replay candle close is `initial_capital × close / first_active_open`.
- Benchmark is a raw underlying return reference and does not apply strategy fees, spread, slippage, latency, or partial-fill assumptions.
- Strategy equity comes from the canonical persisted Backtest equity snapshots.
- Therefore, when the strategy has no market exposure, its equity may be flat while Buy & Hold continues moving. This contrast is intentional and must remain visible.

## Rules

- Use the completed canonical Backtest run as the strategy source of truth.
- Use complete run coverage; do not silently sample or truncate requested series.
- Visual analysis must work for the current 1m / 1h / 1d replay intervals.
- The page may derive visualization-only series such as drawdown and Buy & Hold from complete run market data, but it must not duplicate strategy or execution logic.
- Every chart series must have a visible name, unit, and distinct legend entry.
- Exact fill audit remains available below the charts.
- No change to `StaticGridStrategy` behavior in this phase.
- No Paper or Live trading functionality is added.

- [ ] Calculate signed position quantity and gross exposure percentage over time from persisted position events and replay prices.
